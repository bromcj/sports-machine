## 13. What to be most skeptical of in this codebase

**The thing I would attack first is not anything I wrote this session — it is
the assumption that the gates are measuring what their names say.** Gate 2 has
now graded exactly **one** bet in the project's history, and `coverage` came
back at **0.125**. Every threshold in `model/validation.py` was calibrated by
simulation against an assumed nightly cadence and an assumed CLV spread, and
neither assumption has ever been checked against real graded bets, because until
today there were none. `CLOSING_WINDOW_MIN = 60` is documented as unachievable
with the current schedule — `cronstatus` shows pulls landing **in-play** — so
the gate that is supposed to prove edge is being fed by a collection schedule
that structurally cannot feed it. The comment in `bets/log.py` says so plainly
and says not to widen the window; nobody has fixed the schedule instead. Second
to that: `feeds.odds_twin` is the join the entire system stands on, its
correctness is asserted by a 180-minute window chosen after two wrong guesses,
and the golden test pins its *current* output rather than its *correctness* —
if it has been matching some games wrongly all along, the baseline now enshrines
that. Third: every result in `docs/` traces back to `fair_prob`, and while
Phase 3's control gives me real confidence in it, that control and `fair_prob`
share `bets/engine.novig_probs` — so a bug *inside* proportional de-vigging
would be invisible to both.
