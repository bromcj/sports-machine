## The three things that mattered most

### 1. `cronstatus.py` was crashing in production, and had been

`commence_time` had its `Z` stripped, making it naive, while `ts` carries
`+00:00` and parses aware — subtracting one from the other raises `TypeError`
and takes the whole command down. It is **one of the four commands the owner
actually uses**. Nothing caught it: `cronstatus` has no test and the audit only
imports it. **Running every command once, which is all 1.5c asks for, found it
in ninety seconds.** Fixed, verified on live data.

### 2. The sharp-vs-soft control passes, and that validates everything else

It is +EV by construction, so a negative result would mean the fair-line or
grading path is broken. Reimplemented independently against the four-book
archive on E4's own terms:

|---|---|---|
| DraftKings | −4.21% | −4.24% |
| FanDuel | −3.79% | −3.81% |
| BetMGM | −4.35% | −4.40% |
| games offering >1.5% EV | 1 in 105 | 1 in 104 |

**Two independent implementations agreeing to four hundredths of a percent.**
Every number in this project that leans on `fair_prob` is sound.

### 3. Gate 2 is running for the first time

## Judgment calls, per the Autonomy section

**1.4's package rename was not done.** Moving everything into `sports_machine/`
would re-point both workflows, both `.bat` files, Task Scheduler and prod at new
entry points. The golden test proves *behaviour*; it cannot prove an *entry
point still resolves*, and the brief names the risk — "a broken scheduled job is
a silent outage" — which would land on a machine that has just started a
time-sensitive collection that cannot be re-bought at this price. The low-risk
parts were done. **This is the largest single item left.**

**The 15% prose target was not met: production is 23.8%, from 24.0%.** Reaching
15% means cutting ~900 further lines, and the largest blocks are not history —
they are the reasoning beside a constant, which CLAUDE.md explicitly says to
keep. The brief and the repo's own rules pull opposite ways. I moved the history
and the simulation tables and report the real number.

**The collection schedule is five identical daily triggers, not the brief's
league-specific times.** The events list is free and an idle run spends nothing,
so the collector decides. A schedule that must track two league calendars, bye
weeks and flexed kickoffs drifts out of step silently; this one cannot.

**`backfill.py:46` was left alone.** It reads `abstractGameState` directly
instead of `feeds.stats_api_status`, and `abstractGameState` is `"Final"` for a
postponed game too — the bug that put 88 postponed games in the training set.
Changing it is a logic edit and needs a measurement first. In
`docs/cleanup-findings.md` with everything else left alone.

**Phase 2 did not rebuild the `bets` table.** `model_prob`, `novig_market_prob`,
`edge` and `kelly_fraction` are `NOT NULL` and a manual bet has none of them.
Rather than a destructive ALTER on the one table that records money, both
probabilities are the fair price at entry, so `edge` is exactly **0.0** — at
entry a manual bet claims no edge, because no claim was made.

## One correction to a note this project was carrying

"A named bookmaker list counts as one region however long it is." **True only
within a region.** Measured: 11 NJ books + Pinnacle cost **2** credits per
market, the 4 offshore books alone cost **1**, all 15 cost **2**.

Cost is the number of **regions** spanned. Pinnacle is `eu`; the US books are
`us`/`us2`. Asking for the sharp anchor **doubles every request** — and once
doubled the extra books are free. Every Phase 0 cost carries the ×2.
