## Where we are

Three markets tested against real de-vigged prices with pre-registered rules, three nulls: MLB moneylines, NFL sides, NFL receiving props. Six market-bias hypotheses screened, zero passed; E1 says the market's weights on the obvious baseball variables match reality's. Across four books there's about one shoppable price per hundred games. The one positive signal (the MLB model predicts morning-to-close movement, t = +6.5) is worth a twelfth of the vig.

Two things follow:

1. **No more forecasters on the same inputs.** Not MLB strikeouts, not an NBA model, not the NFL opener test. Out of scope until something changes the picture.
2. **What we built has value anyway: it grades any bet honestly.** Fair close, EV after vig, the shop/info split, closing window, coverage, pre-registration. The next use of the machine is as a scoreboard for anything that gets bet. But first it gets cleaned up and made coherent, because about 5,000 lines landed in a week across ~45 commits, and the parts were written faster than they were reconciled.

## Phase 1: Cleanup and integration

Two goals, and they're different. **Cleanup** removes bloat without changing any result. **Integration** makes every part of the machine agree with every other part: code with schema, docs with code, scripts with entry points, dashboards and monitors with the tables that exist now. A week of fast work leaves seams, and this is where they get closed.

### Ground rules for the whole phase

- **Behavior-preserving, proven by a golden test** (1.2) that runs before and after every commit. The one deliberate exception is 1.6, where the dashboard's *facts* are brought current; its numbers are recomputed from the same sources, not typed in.
- **Read-only against live data.** Point `SPORTS_MACHINE_DATA_DIR` at a **copy** of `data/` taken by `python backup.py`. Never touch the live database, `archive/`, `validation.json` or the scheduled task until the final merge step.
- **No logic edits.** No gate, schema, threshold or algorithm changes. Moving a function is fine; changing what it computes is not. Anything that looks wrong goes in `docs/cleanup-findings.md` with file:line and is left alone.
- **One commit per move**, each passing `pytest`, `python audit.py` and the golden test, with a message that says what moved where.
- **Don't delete an audit check that touches live data.** Only checks that duplicate a pure-function pytest test may move to `tests/`.

## Phase 2: The scoreboard: grade any bet the same way

Add `mode='manual'` and make entering a bet take under thirty seconds, or it won't get used.

1. **Entry.** A one-line command and a CSV drop folder, both accepting: sport, date, matchup or game id, market (`h2h`, `spread`, `total`, or a prop key plus player), side, line, price taken, book, stake, optional tag (`boost`, `promo`, `research`, `sgp-leg`). Resolve the game with `feeds`; refuse anything unresolved; refuse a bet whose game has started.
2. **At entry time, record what was available:** the best price across held books at that moment and the fair price (Pinnacle no-vig, else consensus, recorded which). That's what makes the shop/info split possible for a human's picks.
3. **Grading.** Identical to paper bets: settle from results where we hold them (sides and totals from scores; props from box scores where collected, otherwise a manual result field, flagged), `ev_fair_close`, `shop`, `info`, the 60-minute window, coverage. Boosted prices are graded against the same fair close, so a boost's real value is visible.
4. **Reports.** `python -m sports_machine scoreboard`: by tag, sport, market and bet type: n, ROI with a bootstrap CI, average `ev_fair_close`, `shop` vs `info`, coverage, and the same three-gate verdict the model gets (50+ graded, `info` clear of zero by 3 SE, coverage ≥ 75%). The question it answers is "is this handicapping worth continuing?", held to the standard the model was.
5. Tests for entry validation, the void rule and the report math. Add the new mode and command to the integration matrix, COMMANDS.md and the state doc in the same commit; the matrix script should fail if a new mode isn't documented.

**Report point 3: a demo with three fake bets, their grading, and the scoreboard output, in `docs/reports/phase2.md`; then delete the fake bets.**
